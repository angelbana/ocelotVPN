Application icon, tray icons and installer images
-------------------------------------------------

Taken from the "virtual private network vpn symbol" icon:
https://icon-icons.com/icon/virtual-private-network-vpn-symbol/72481

By Google, from the Google Material Design Icons pack.
Licence: CC BY 4.0 - https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

That licence allows the artwork to be used commercially and redistributed,
including inside the installer and the app image, and asks
for one thing in return: that the author and the licence are named. The
credit is therefore given in the program's About window as well as here -
a line in a file under docs/ is not something the people who receive the
program ever see.

The Windows application icon and the tray icons are rendered from the coloured
artwork.
