The program's own icon
----------------------

The ocelot on the icon is the one the program draws in its window, rendered
from src/qml/Mascot.qml rather than kept as a second copy of the artwork. It is
produced by loading that file into a warm disc and saving the result at every
size Windows asks for (16 through 256), which are then packed into
src/ocelot.ico; the 256 one is also src/Resources/ocelot.png, which is what a
Linux desktop shows.


The icons in the notification area
----------------------------------

Taken from the "virtual private network vpn symbol" icon:
https://icon-icons.com/icon/virtual-private-network-vpn-symbol/72481

By Google, from the Google Material Design Icons pack.
Licence: CC BY 4.0 - https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

That licence allows the artwork to be used commercially and redistributed,
including inside the installer and the app image, and asks for one thing in
return: that the author and the licence are named. The credit is therefore
given in the program's About window as well as here - a line in a file under
docs/ is not something the people who receive the program ever see.
